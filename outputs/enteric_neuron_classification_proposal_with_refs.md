# Consensus Multi-Inheritance Classification of Mammalian Enteric Neurons (with references)

## Source
Based on: "Comprehensive Classifications of Mammalian Enteric Neurons Across Functional, Morphological, and Molecular Modalities" (synthesizing >30 primary sources)

---

## Classification Axes

Enteric neurons are classified along four independent axes:

| Axis | Classes |
|------|---------|
| **A. Morphology** | Dogiel type I, Dogiel type II |
| **B. Electrophysiology** | S-type, AH-type |
| **C. Function** | IPAN, excitatory motor neuron, inhibitory motor neuron, ascending interneuron, descending interneuron, secretomotor neuron, vasodilator neuron, intestinofugal neuron |
| **D. Neurotransmitter** | Cholinergic (ChAT+), nitrergic (NOS1+), VIPergic, dopaminergic, serotonergic |

**Refs — axis definitions:**
- Axis A: Dogiel 1896/1899 via Sayegh et al. 2013; Magalhaes et al. 2021; Dharshika et al. 2022; Brehmer 2021
- Axis B: Hirst et al. 1974 via Fung et al. 2020; Sayegh et al. 2013; Zhao et al. 2023
- Axis C: Furness 2006/2012 via Ren et al. 2008; Jakob et al. 2020; Bosi et al. 2020; Natale et al. 2021; Majd et al. 2024
- Axis D: Magalhaes et al. 2021; Jiang et al. 2017; Morarach et al. 2020; Zeisel et al. 2018

## Axis-Axis Correspondence Matrix

| Functional class (Axis C) | Morphology (A) | Electrophys. (B) | Primary transmitter (D) | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| IPAN | Dogiel II (**1:1**) | AH-type (**1:1**) | Cholinergic + substance P + CGRP (**1:1**) | Very high |
| Excitatory motor neuron | Dogiel I (**1:1**) | S-type (**1:1**) | Cholinergic + substance P (**1:1**) | Very high |
| Inhibitory motor neuron | Dogiel I (**1:1**) | S-type (**1:1**) | Nitrergic + VIP + ATP (**1:1**) | Very high |
| Ascending interneuron | Dogiel I (**1:1**) | S-type (**1:1**) | Cholinergic + tachykinins (**1:1**) | High |
| Descending interneuron | Dogiel I (**1:1**) | S-type (**1:1**) | **partial** (ACh *or* NO/VIP/ATP) | High for morph/ephys; partial for transmitter |
| Cholinergic secretomotor neuron | Dogiel I (**1:1**) | S-type (**1:1**) | Cholinergic + substance P (**1:1**) | High |
| Non-chol. secretomotor/vasodilator | Dogiel I (**1:1**) | S-type (**1:1**) | VIPergic + dynorphin + galanin (**1:1**) | High |
| Intestinofugal neuron | Dogiel I (**1:1**) | S-type (**1:1**) | **partial** (varies) | High for morph/ephys; low for transmitter |

**Refs — correspondence evidence:**
- IPAN = Dogiel II = AH-type: Dharshika et al. 2022; Kuwahara et al. 2020; Zhao et al. 2023; Fung et al. 2020; Bosi et al. 2020; Sayegh et al. 2013; Sun et al. 2025; Gomez-Frittelli et al. 2024, 2025

- Descending IN transmitter heterogeneity: Ren et al. 2008; Jamka et al. 2024; Natale et al. 2021 (1 ascending + ≥3 descending subtypes)
- Secretomotor/vasodilator: Ren et al. 2008; Petto et al. 2015; Makowska et al. 2019
- Intestinofugal: Ren et al. 2008; Mogor et al. 2021; Nikolla et al. 2025

### Axis A × Axis B (Morphology × Electrophysiology)

| | AH-type | S-type |
|---|---|---|
| **Dogiel type II** | **IPAN** (safe cross-product) | — (not observed) |
| **Dogiel type I** | — (not observed) | **All non-IPAN classes** (safe cross-product) |

This 2×2 is effectively diagonal: the two axis pairs are nearly synonymous in the ENS. This is the single strongest cross-axis correspondence in the literature.

**Refs:** Dharshika et al. 2022; Kuwahara et al. 2020; Sayegh et al. 2013; Fung et al. 2020; Zhao et al. 2023; Brehmer 2021

## Proposed Cross-Product Terms

Below, each proposed term crosses exactly two or more axes where the correspondence matrix shows a **1:1** or near-1:1 mapping. Terms are grouped by the number of axes crossed.

### Two-axis cross-products (Morphology × Electrophysiology)

These capture the strongest single correlation in the ENS. Only two terms are needed:

#### 1. Dogiel type II AH-type enteric neuron

- **Axiom:** Dogiel type II ∩ AH-type
- **Definition:** An enteric neuron with Dogiel type II morphology (large round/oval soma, multiple long smooth processes) and AH-type electrophysiology (prolonged afterhyperpolarization >1-2 s, inflection on falling phase of action potential). This combination is characteristic of intrinsic primary afferent neurons across all mammalian species studied.
- **Refs:** Dharshika et al. 2022; Kuwahara et al. 2020; Fung et al. 2020; Zhao et al. 2023; Brehmer 2021; Sayegh et al. 2013; Gomez-Frittelli et al. 2024, 2025

#### 2. Dogiel type I S-type enteric neuron

- **Axiom:** Dogiel type I ∩ S-type
- **Definition:** An enteric neuron with Dogiel type I morphology (flattened angular soma, single axon, short lamellar dendrites) and S-type electrophysiology (brief action potentials, fast EPSPs, no slow AHP). This combination encompasses motor neurons, interneurons, secretomotor neurons, vasodilator neurons, and intestinofugal neurons.
- **Refs:** Dharshika et al. 2022; Kuwahara et al. 2020; Ren et al. 2008; Sayegh et al. 2013; Savulescu-Fiedler et al. 2025

### Three- and four-axis cross-products

#### IPANs (Function × Dogiel II × AH-type × Cholinergic)

Safe because: Dogiel II + AH-type + cholinergic + IPAN function is a **1:1:1:1** mapping across all four axes. Every source agrees.

**General IPAN refs:** Ren et al. 2008; Fung et al. 2020; Bosi et al. 2020; Kuwahara et al. 2020; Magalhaes et al. 2021; Dharshika et al. 2022; Zhao et al. 2023; Sun et al. 2025; Jamka et al. 2024

#### 3. Myenteric intrinsic primary afferent neuron

- **Axes crossed:** A×B×C×D + location (4+)
- **Definition:** A Dogiel type II, AH-type, cholinergic intrinsic primary afferent neuron with soma in the myenteric plexus, detecting chemical and mechanical stimuli from the gut wall. Represents ~30% of myenteric neurons. Expresses Calca/Calcb, substance P, and CGRP.
- **Refs:** Magalhaes et al. 2021 (~30% of myenteric plexus); Fung et al. 2020 (myenteric vs submucosal division); Morarach et al. 2020 (ENC6/7/12 molecular subtypes); Sun et al. 2025 (somata predominantly myenteric)

#### 4. Submucosal intrinsic primary afferent neuron

- **Axes crossed:** A×B×C×D + location (4+)
- **Definition:** A Dogiel type II, AH-type, cholinergic intrinsic primary afferent neuron with soma in the submucosal plexus, uniquely sensitive to subtle mechanical stimulation of the mucosa. Represents ~14% of submucosal neurons.
- **Refs:** Magalhaes et al. 2021 (~14% of submucosal plexus); Fung et al. 2020 ("only the former subset [submucosal] are sensitive to subtle mechanical stimulation of the mucosa"); Ren et al. 2008 (cell bodies in both plexuses)

#### Excitatory motor neurons (Function × Dogiel I × S-type × Cholinergic)

Safe because: excitatory motor neuron + Dogiel I + S-type + cholinergic is a **1:1:1:1** mapping. Circular vs longitudinal target is a further differentia supported by retrograde tracing.

**General excitatory MN refs:** Ren et al. 2008; Jiang et al. 2017; Dharshika et al. 2022; Natale et al. 2021; Marinova et al. 2022

#### 5. Cholinergic excitatory motor neuron to circular muscle

- **Axes crossed:** A×B×C×D + target (4+)
- **Definition:** A Dogiel type I, S-type, cholinergic motor neuron of the myenteric plexus that innervates the circular smooth muscle layer, causing contraction via acetylcholine and substance P.
- **Refs:** Ren et al. 2008 ("four functional classes of motor neuron... inhibitory or excitatory motor neurons to either the circular or longitudinal smooth muscle layers"); Jiang et al. 2017 ("Acetylcholine and substance P are the major neurotransmitters of excitatory motor neurons"); Morarach et al. 2020 (ENC1-4 = excitatory MN, Tac1/Calb2)

#### 6. Cholinergic excitatory motor neuron to longitudinal muscle

- **Axes crossed:** A×B×C×D + target (4+)
- **Definition:** A Dogiel type I, S-type, cholinergic motor neuron of the myenteric plexus that innervates the longitudinal smooth muscle layer, causing contraction via acetylcholine and substance P.
- **Refs:** Ren et al. 2008 (four classes to circular/longitudinal); Bubeck et al. 2023 ("excitatory and inhibitory motor neurons innervate both the circular and longitudinal muscles"); Natale et al. 2021 ("excitatory cholinergic neurons... each subtype encompasses approximately 5-10% of all enteric neurons within the myenteric plexus")

#### Inhibitory motor neurons (Function × Dogiel I × S-type × Nitrergic)

Safe because: inhibitory motor neuron + Dogiel I + S-type + nitrergic is a **1:1:1:1** mapping. Every source identifies NOS1/VIP as the inhibitory motor neuron signature.

**General inhibitory MN refs:** Ren et al. 2008; Costa et al. 1992; Jiang et al. 2017; Gallego et al. 2016; Natale et al. 2021

#### 7. Nitrergic inhibitory motor neuron to circular muscle

- **Axes crossed:** A×B×C×D + target (4+)
- **Definition:** A Dogiel type I, S-type, nitrergic motor neuron of the myenteric plexus that innervates the circular smooth muscle layer, causing relaxation via nitric oxide, VIP, and ATP as co-transmitter. Immunoreactive for NOS1 and VIP.
- **Refs:** Ren et al. 2008 ("inhibitory motor neurons to the circular muscle are immunoreactive for NOS and VIP and use ATP as a coneurotransmitter"); Costa et al. 1992 ("nitric oxide synthase is located in a sub-population of enteric neurons, amongst which are inhibitory motor neurons that supply the circular muscle layer"); Morarach et al. 2020 (ENC8-9 = Nos1/Gal/Vip/Npy)

#### 8. Nitrergic inhibitory motor neuron to longitudinal muscle

- **Axes crossed:** A×B×C×D + target (4+)
- **Definition:** A Dogiel type I, S-type, nitrergic motor neuron of the myenteric plexus that innervates the longitudinal smooth muscle layer, causing relaxation via nitric oxide and VIP.
- **Refs:** Ren et al. 2008 (four classes including to longitudinal muscle); Bubeck et al. 2023; Gallego et al. 2016 ("Nitric oxide (NO) and ATP or a related purine represent the primary inhibitory neurotransmitters")

#### Ascending interneurons (Function × Dogiel I × S-type × Cholinergic)

Safe because: ascending interneuron + Dogiel I + S-type + cholinergic/tachykininergic is a **1:1:1:1** mapping.

#### 9. Cholinergic ascending interneuron

- **Axes crossed:** A×B×C×D (4)
- **Definition:** A Dogiel type I, S-type, cholinergic interneuron of the myenteric plexus with an orally directed axon, using acetylcholine and tachykinins to relay ascending reflex signals in peristaltic circuits. One subtype in most regions; two subtypes in the proximal colon.
- **Refs:** Ren et al. 2008 ("one subtype of interneuron (two types in proximal colon) with an ascending projection"); Jamka et al. 2024 ("ascending interneurons typically use acetylcholine and tachykinins as neurotransmitters"); Marinova et al. 2022 ("Most of the ascending enteric interneurons belong to the Dogiel type I neurons")

#### Secretomotor/vasodilator neurons (Function × Dogiel I × S-type × transmitter)

Safe because: both types show **1:1** mapping of function to neurotransmitter identity, and both are uniformly Dogiel I / S-type.

**General secretomotor/vasodilator refs:** Ren et al. 2008; Petto et al. 2015; Makowska et al. 2019; Kuwahara et al. 2020

#### 10. Cholinergic secretomotor neuron

- **Axes crossed:** A×B×C×D + location (4+)
- **Definition:** A Dogiel type I, S-type, cholinergic neuron of the submucosal plexus that innervates secretory epithelium, stimulating water and electrolyte secretion via acetylcholine and substance P. ~42% of inner submucosal plexus neurons in pigs; ~66% co-localize substance P.
- **Refs:** Ren et al. 2008 ("two types of excitatory secretomotor neurons going to the epithelium"); Petto et al. 2015 ("42% of all neurons expressed ChAT. About 66% of ChAT-positive neurons co-localized SP"); Makowska et al. 2019 (ISP neurochemistry)

#### 11. VIPergic secretomotor/vasodilator neuron

- **Axes crossed:** A×B×C×D + location (4+)
- **Definition:** A Dogiel type I, S-type, non-cholinergic neuron of the submucosal plexus immunoreactive for VIP, dynorphin, and galanin, that innervates both secretory epithelium and submucosal arterioles. Comprises ~45% of submucosal neurons. May use ATP as co-transmitter.
- **Refs:** Ren et al. 2008 ("Noncholinergic secretomotor/vasodilator neurons are immunoreactive for dynorphin, galanin, and VIP. These neurons account for ~45% of submucosal neurons"); Petto et al. 2015 (nNOS/VIP co-localization in OSP); Llorente 2024 ("Submucosal neurons... mainly consist of cholinergic neurons and VIP-expressing noncholinergic neurons")

#### Intestinofugal neuron

#### 12. Intestinofugal neuron

- **Axes crossed:** A×B×C + location (3) — transmitter axis not crossed due to incomplete characterisation
- **Definition:** A Dogiel type I, S-type enteric neuron of the myenteric plexus that sends axonal projections outside the gut wall to prevertebral sympathetic ganglia, enabling enteric modulation of sympathetic reflex pathways to the gallbladder, pancreas, and other visceral targets.
- **Refs:** Ren et al. 2008 ("a small number of intestinofugal neurons that send a projection to prevertebral ganglia"); Mogor et al. 2021; Nikolla et al. 2025 ("intestinofugal neurons (IFANs), a smaller population of myenteric neurons that project to the gallbladder, pancreas, and central nervous system")

#### Transcriptomic IPAN subtypes (all axes + scRNA-seq)

Only proposed where independent electrophysiological + morphological validation exists beyond scRNA-seq.

#### 13. Myenteric IPAN, Nmu-subtype (ENC6)

- **Axes crossed:** All + transcriptomic
- **Definition:** A Dogiel type II, AH-type, cholinergic intrinsic primary afferent neuron of the myenteric plexus that selectively expresses neuromedin U (Nmu) and cadherin 6 (Cdh6), and detects chemical and mechanical stimuli in the gut wall. Also expresses Calcb. Projects mainly circumferentially and branches extensively in myenteric ganglia. Corresponds to ENC6 of the Morarach et al. (2020) molecular taxonomy.
- **Refs:** Morarach et al. 2020 ("ENC6... mapped to IPANs... clearly separable by their selective expression of Nmu"); Gomez-Frittelli et al. 2024, 2025 ("Cdh6+ neurons demonstrate all other distinguishing classifications of enteric sensory neurons including marker expression of Calcb and Nmu, Dogiel type II morphology and AH-type electrophysiology and IH current"; "Cdh6 neurons express IPAN markers Calcb and Nmu. Sparse labeling of individual IPANs reveals they project mainly circumferentially and branch extensively in myenteric ganglia")

#### 14. Myenteric IPAN, Nxph2-subtype (ENC12)

- **Axes crossed:** All + transcriptomic
- **Definition:** A Dogiel type II, AH-type, cholinergic intrinsic primary afferent neuron of the myenteric plexus that selectively expresses neurexophilin-2 (Nxph2). Also expresses Calca/Calcb. Corresponds to ENC12 of the Morarach et al. (2020) molecular taxonomy.
- **Refs:** Morarach et al. 2020 ("ENC12 (neurexophilin-2; Nxph2)"; "ENC6, 7 and 12 were mapped to IPANs and expressed different combinations of previously defined markers (Calca/Calcb/Nfem/Calb1/Calb2). These plausible IPANs were clearly separable by their selective expression of Nmu, Ucn-3/Cck or Nxph2")
- **Note:** Independent electrophysiological validation less complete than for Nmu/Cdh6+ subtype; included on strength of molecular + morphological evidence from Morarach et al. 2020.

## Cross-Products to AVOID (Unsafe)

| Proposed combination | Why unsafe | Refs |
|---|---|---|
| Descending interneuron × specific transmitter | At least 3 neurochemically distinct subtypes of descending interneurons exist (cholinergic, NOS/VIP/ATP, and others). The function-transmitter mapping is **1:many**, not 1:1. | Ren et al. 2008 ("at least three subtypes of interneuron with a descending projection"); Jamka et al. 2024 ("descending which transmits information in the aboral direction" using "combinations of acetylcholine, nitric oxide, VIP, and ATP"); Natale et al. 2021 ("three types of anally directed descending interneurons") |
| Intestinofugal neuron × specific transmitter | Neurotransmitter identity not consistently characterised across sources. | Ren et al. 2008; Mogor et al. 2021 |
| Dopaminergic × morphology or electrophysiology | Morphological and electrophysiological properties of dopaminergic enteric neurons are not resolved. Some co-express ChAT, others express canonical DA markers only. Subtyping is still in flux. | Li et al. 2003 (developmental lineage); Pradeloux et al. 2025 (10-20% of ENS, oral-to-aboral gradient); Recinto et al. 2023 ("distinct subtypes of DAT-tdTomato neurons expressing co-transmitters... some of them likely co-releasing acetylcholine, while others were positive for a slew of canonical DA markers (TH, VMAT2 and GIRK2)... a seemingly novel population... ChAT/DAT-tdTomato-immunoreactive neurons characterised by the expression of Grp, Calcb and Sst") |
| ENC1-4 as individual CL terms | These four excitatory motor neuron subtypes differ by combinatorial gene expression (Calb2, Ndufa4l2, Gda, Penk, Fut9) but their functional differentiation (e.g., which target muscle layer) is not yet mapped. | Morarach et al. 2020 ("ENC1-4 displayed a partly shared gene expression, including Tac1, but differed in their expression of Calb2, Ndufa4l2, Gda, Penk and Fut9"; "ENC1-4 were assigned to excitatory motor neurons") |
| ENC8 vs ENC9 as individual CL terms | Both are NOS1+ inhibitory motor neurons differing in NPY/Cox8b levels. Functional distinction not yet clear. | Morarach et al. 2020 ("ENC8-9 jointly expressed Nos1 and c1ql1, but Npy and Cox8b were significantly lower in ENC9") |
| ENC7 (Cck/Ucn3 IPAN) as individual CL term | Molecular evidence strong but independent electrophysiological/morphological validation less complete than for ENC6 (Nmu+/Cdh6+). | Morarach et al. 2020 (molecular evidence only); cf. Gomez-Frittelli et al. 2024, 2025 (validation exists for Nmu+ but not specifically for Cck/Ucn3+ subtype) |

## Notes for Ontology Implementation

1. **Morphology × Electrophysiology equivalence**: The Dogiel II / AH-type and Dogiel I / S-type pairings are so tight that they could be modelled as logical equivalence axioms (Dogiel type II enteric neuron EquivalentTo AH-type enteric neuron). However, modelling them as independent classes with an empirically supported SubClassOf relationship is safer, as rare exceptions may exist. **Refs:** Brehmer 2021; Dharshika et al. 2022; Kuwahara et al. 2020.

2. **Dual-function classes**: The VIPergic secretomotor/vasodilator neuron legitimately inherits from both `enteric secretomotor neuron` and `enteric vasodilator neuron`. This is a genuine multi-inheritance case, not an artefact. **Ref:** Ren et al. 2008.

3. **Plexus location**: Model using `has_soma_location` some `myenteric plexus` / `submucosal plexus` rather than as a separate classification axis. Location is a differentia within functional types (especially IPANs), not an independent classification. **Ref:** Fung et al. 2020 (myenteric vs submucosal IPAN functional distinction).

4. **Transcriptomic subtypes**: The ENC classes from Morarach et al. (2020) are mouse-specific. Cross-product terms at the ENC level should carry a taxon restriction (NCBITaxon:10090). Only add as CL terms when independently validated by electrophysiology or functional assay — currently met for ENC6 (Nmu+/Cdh6+ IPANs: Gomez-Frittelli et al. 2024, 2025) and partially for ENC12 (Nxph2+ IPANs: Morarach et al. 2020).

5. **Species scope**: All 14 proposed cross-product terms above are supported across multiple mammalian species (guinea pig, mouse, human) unless noted. Taxon-restrict where evidence is single-species. **Refs for cross-species conservation:** Dharshika et al. 2022; May-Zhang et al. 2020; Bianco et al. 2021. **Refs for species-specific caveats:** May-Zhang et al. 2020 ("some EN subtype-specific genes from mice are expressed in completely distinct morphologically defined subtypes in humans"); Pradeloux et al. 2025 (dopaminergic neuron proportions differ mouse vs human).
