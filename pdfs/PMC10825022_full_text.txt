## Key Findings

### Human Viscerofugal Neuron (VFN) Characterization
- First characterization of VFNs in human colon
- 903 VFN cell bodies labeled from 25 preparations (n=23 patients)
- 85% had axons projecting orally before entering colonic nerves
- 97% were uniaxonal

### Neurochemical Markers (215 VFNs studied in detail, n=6)
- ChAT: 89% (191/215)
- NOS: 13% (29/215)
- Calbindin: 13% (27/215)
- Enkephalin: 9% (20/215)
- Substance P: 7% (14/215)
- Calretinin: 3% (7/215)
- VIP: 2% (4/215)
- SOM: 1% (3/215)
- 5-HT, CGRP, NPY: 0%
- **CART: 0% (0 of 123 VFNs tested)**

### Critical Finding on CART
"A recent study has reported that VFNs in the mouse are immunoreactive for Cocaine and Amphetamine-Regulated Transcript (CART) (Muller et al., 2020). We tested CART immunoreactivity in 3 preparations of human colon (n = 3; 1 ascending colon, 2 descending colon) after filling VFNs with biotinamide applied to colonic nerves. Of 123 VFN cell bodies labeled, none was immunoreactive for CART, although CART-immunoreactive cell bodies and axons were present in most ganglia."

"The absence of CART from a sample of 123 VFN cell bodies (n = 3 patients) is in contrast to the mouse, where in both small and large intestines VFNs are substantially CART-immunoreactive (Muller et al., 2020). All of these variations in expression reinforce the extent of inter-species differences and indicate the importance of direct analysis of human tissue, wherever possible."

### Morphological Types
- Lamellar/stubby: 41.9% (90/215)
- Filamentous/spiny: 35.8% (77/215)
- Simple: 19.1% (41/215)
- Dogiel type II: 3.3% (7/215)

### Chemical Coding Overlap with Other Neuron Types
- 68.8% of VFNs had chemical codes matching EMNs (excitatory motor neurons)
- Only 7.4% matched IMN codes
- Only 6.0% matched DIN codes
- "VFNs shared chemical coding with all 5 major types of myenteric neurons"

### Conclusion
"Human VFNs are principally cholinergic neurons with a single axon that projects orally along the gut before exiting through colonic nerves. Most are surrounded by baskets of axonal varicosities arising from other enteric neurons, including those in ascending neural pathways."

## Implications for CL Curation
1. CART/CARTPT is NOT a reliable marker for human intestinofugal neurons (0% in human vs. high in mouse)
2. Human intestinofugal neurons are primarily ChAT+ (89%)
3. The defining feature remains the extra-intestinal projection to prevertebral ganglia
4. Chemical coding in human VFNs overlaps extensively with EMNs - no unique marker distinguishes VFNs
